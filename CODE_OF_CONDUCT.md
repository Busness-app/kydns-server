# Code of Conduct

KyDNS contributors must keep project spaces welcoming, specific, and focused
on the work. Disagreement about a design is useful; attacks on people are not.

## Expected behavior

- Assume good intent and discuss evidence.
- Give specific, actionable feedback.
- Respect names, pronouns, identities, and boundaries.
- Credit contributions, including AI assistance where applicable.
- Protect private information and security reports.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized attention.
- Slurs, personal attacks, deliberate misgendering, or doxxing.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern or vulnerability.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer. Include what
happened, where and when it happened, and any relevant links. Reports are
handled confidentially.

Report security vulnerabilities through the process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not in a public issue.

## Scope

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation,
commits, chats, and other spaces where a person represents KyDNS.
